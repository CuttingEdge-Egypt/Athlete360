# Product Overview

**Athlete360** is a comprehensive AI-powered sports analytics platform designed for athletes, coaches, and teams. It delivers a complete 360-degree view of athlete performance, strategic insights, and personalized recommendations.

**Sport Coverage:**

- **Taekwondo** (التايكواندو): Full-featured platform with complete analytics, training, and competition management
- **Other Sports**: Core analytics capabilities including Analytics360 and AI Video Analysis
    - Tennis
    - Table Tennis
    - Badminton
    - Boxing
    - Wrestling
    - Judo
    - Karate
    - Shooting
    - Squash


# Mission Statement

To enhance athlete performance, optimize training, and minimize injury risks through cutting-edge AI technology and data-driven insights, starting with comprehensive Taekwondo support and expanding to other sports.

---

# Target Audience

- **Primary**: Competitive Taekwondo athletes (amateur to elite level)
- **Secondary**: Athletes in other sports seeking AI-powered video and performance analytics
- **Tertiary**: Coaches, national federations, sports academies, and team managers


# Key Features & Value Propositions

## Full Feature Set (Taekwondo)

1. Athlete 360° Profile

Complete performance overview including rankings, achievements, historical data, and AI-generated strengths/weaknesses analysis. Track your journey from amateur to champion.

1. AI Video Analysis

Upload match footage and receive detailed AI-powered breakdowns of techniques, scoring patterns, kicks, punches, and tactical violations. Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash technology.

1. Intelligent Chat Assistant

Ask questions, get personalized insights, and generate custom training plans through natural conversation with our GPT-4o powered AI assistant.

1. Opponent Analysis

Know your competition. Get strategic insights on opponents based on their fighting style, tendencies, and historical performance data.

1. Training & Nutrition Planning

AI-generated personalized training programs and nutrition plans based on sports science, including TDEE calculations, macro optimization, and competition preparation schedules.

1. Rank-Up Calculator

Strategic guidance on how to advance in world rankings with clear, actionable steps and competition recommendations.

1. Competition Management

Sync data from live competitions, track tournament brackets, and access comprehensive competition databases with real-time updates.


## Core Features (Other Sports)

1. Analytics360

Comprehensive performance analytics and AI-generated insights on strengths, weaknesses, and performance trends.

1. AI Video Analysis

Upload match/game footage and receive detailed AI-powered breakdowns of techniques, patterns, and tactical elements specific to your sport.


# Competitive Differentiators

- **Taekwondo-First**: Purpose-built with complete feature set for Taekwondo, expandable to other sports
- **AI-First**: Dual AI engine combining advanced language models with cutting-edge video analysis technology
- **Sport-Adaptive**: Core analytics work across multiple sports disciplines
- **Global Database**: Integration with world ranking systems and competition APIs (Taekwondo)
- **Multi-Language**: English and Arabic support with RTL/LTR optimization
- **Mobile-Ready**: Progressive Web App for offline access and push notifications
- **Data Privacy**: Secure authentication with role-based access controls
