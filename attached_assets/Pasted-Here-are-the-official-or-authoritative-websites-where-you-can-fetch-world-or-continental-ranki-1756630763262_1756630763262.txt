Here are the **official or authoritative websites** where you can fetch world (or continental) rankings for each sport you listed. These are essential reference sources you can use to integrate into your feature:

---

### Taekwondo

* **World Taekwondo (WT) official athlete rankings**
  Includes continuous, weight-category-specific rankings (e.g., Olympic Kyorugi). ([worldtaekwondo.org][1])
* **World Taekwondo Rankings (older listing)**
  An alternate interface listing current ranking positions. ([worldtaekwondo.org][2])
* **Taekwondo Data – independent database**
  Extensive historical and current ranking data on athletes. ([TaekwondoData][3])

---

### Fencing

* **International Fencing Federation (FIE) – Athletes & Rankings**
  Official world rankings across weapons (foil, épée, sabre). ([fie.org][4])
* **Eurofencing – Individual and team European rankings**
  Continental-level reference for European fencers. ([eurofencing.info][5])
* **British Fencing – national ranking lists**
  Though national, it can support deeper regional data. ([BRITISH FENCING][6])

---

### Wrestling

* **United World Wrestling (UWW) – official rankings**
  Covers freestyle, Greco-Roman, and women's categories, by weight class. ([United World Wrestling][7])
* **FloWrestling – international and championship rankings**
  Up-to-date rankings, including World Championship & Olympic rankings. ([FloWrestling][8])

---

### Squash

* **PSA Squash Tour official site – World Rankings**
  Current Men’s and Women’s world rankings maintained weekly. ([psasquashtour.com][9], [worldsquash.org][10])
* **SquashInfo – PSA World Squash Rankings**
  Detailed snapshot as of August 2025. ([squashinfo.com][11])

---

### Football (Soccer) – European / African

* **FIFA / Coca-Cola Men's World Ranking (FIFA.com)**
  The primary international football ranking system for all national teams. ([Inside FIFA][12], [Wikipedia][13])
* **FIFA Rankings by Confederation (e.g., CAF, UEFA)**
  Offers region-specific rankings (e.g., African zone). ([Football Ranking][14])
* **UEFA Club & Association Coefficient Rankings**
  Useful for European club and country-level strength/ranking context. ([UEFA.com][15])
* **Football-ranking.com – live and daily-updated FIFA rankings**
  Independent source with frequent updates. ([Football Ranking][16])
* **EloRatings.net – alternative ranking model**
  Based on Elo methodology for national teams. ([eloratings.net][17])

---

### Basketball

* **FIBA Men’s (and Women’s) World Rankings**
  Official world rankings for national teams, game-based and updated regularly. ([Wikipedia][18], [Olympics][19])
* **FIBA World Ranking for Boys (Nike-presented)**
  Youth men's ranking by region and world. ([FIBA Basketball][20])
* **Eurobasket – Top 100 club/team rankings**
  Useful for European club-level rankings. ([Eurobasket][21])

### How to Use These Sites in Your Rank-History Feature

* **Fetch Current Rankings**: Scrape or integrate via APIs (if available) from the official ranking pages.
* **Create Timeline Data**: Combine scraped current data with historical snapshots (if you store snapshots over time) to chart rank evolution.
* **Cite as References**: Store the URLs of the ranking pages in your data as sources for transparency and verification.
* **Handle Data Differences**: Recognize differences in ranking methods (e.g., points-based, Elo, competition-based) and clarify in your UI.
